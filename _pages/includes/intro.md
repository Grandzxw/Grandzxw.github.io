# About

I am a **Senior Researcher in World Models at UBTECH Robotics**, working on world models and embodied intelligence. I received my Ph.D. in Robotics from the Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen.

My research interests include embodied world models, world-action models, robot learning, and 3D geometric perception. My earlier work focused on robust LiDAR perception, point-cloud processing, place recognition, and SLAM in adverse weather. I am a core author of **Thinker-WM**, a unified world model that jointly models RGB video, depth, and robot actions.

For research inquiries and collaboration, please contact me at [grandzhaoxw@gmail.com](mailto:grandzhaoxw@gmail.com). You can also find my publications on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=miv8T6MAAAAJ&hl=zh-TW), and my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/Grandzxw).
